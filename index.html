Weekly Logic Puzzle

Three switches outside a room control one bulb inside. You may enter the room only once. How do you determine which switch controls the bulb?

Turn on switch 1, wait a few minutes, turn it off, then turn on switch 2 and enter. Hot-but-off bulb = switch 1. On = switch 2. Cold-and-off = switch 3.
